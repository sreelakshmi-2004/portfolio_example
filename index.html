ACADEMIC RESEARCH PROJECT Up to 97% Accuracy

HistoNet: Oral Cancer Detection Using Deep Learning

Problem Statement & Purpose

Oral cancer is one of the most critical health conditions where early detection drastically improves survival rates. Manual histopathological analysis is time-intensive and demands specialist scrutiny. HistoNet explores deep learning to assist diagnostic efficiency while ensuring clinical transparency through Explainable AI (XAI).

Architecture & Approach

Developed a deep learning-based system for oral cancer detection using histopathological images, achieving up to 97% diagnostic accuracy. To bridge the black-box gap in clinical deployment, integrated Grad-CAM (Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping) and Occlusion Sensitivity, visually verifying that the network focuses on genuine cellular anomalies rather than extraneous image artifacts.
